Privacy Policy

Last updated · 9 June 2026

LOOMUS is a place to read, think, and keep what's yours. We collect as little as we can, we don't sell your data, and your margin — your notes, stars, and the lines you draw — belongs to you.

Who we are

LOOMUS ("we", "us") is operated by LOOM US Corp, alongside the LOOMUS Foundation, a public-interest project for learning in the AI era. For any privacy question, write to sophie@loomus.ai.

What we collect

  • Your email — when you sign in (we send a six-digit code) and, if you opt in, to send the weekly letter from Marginalia.
  • Your reading & your margin — the books you open, concepts you star, thoughts you plant, and lines you draw, so your lifetime library and /you constellation can grow with you.
  • Payment information — if you support LOOMUS or order a Distill, payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Student status — if you claim the student rate, we verify a .edu (or equivalent) email; we don't keep transcripts or IDs.
  • Basic usage data — pages visited and rough device/region, via privacy-respecting analytics, to understand what's working.

How we use it

To run your account and sign you in; to build and show your personal library and constellation; to process payments and student access; to send the weekly letter if you asked for it; and to improve LOOMUS. That's the whole list. We do not use your reading or notes to train external AI models, and we do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that make LOOMUS work, each under their own terms: Stripe (payments), Supabase (accounts & secure storage), Google Analytics (usage metrics), and Netlify / Cloudflare (hosting & delivery). We may disclose data if required by law.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies — essential ones to keep you signed in, plus analytics. No advertising cookies, ever. Details in our Cookie Policy.

How long we keep it

Your margin follows the product's ethos: decay, not deletion — untouched stars dim but aren't erased, because some readings wait. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time, and we will.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and opt out of the weekly letter (every email has an unsubscribe link). If you're in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), those laws give you these rights explicitly — we honor them for everyone. To exercise any right, email sophie@loomus.ai.

Children

LOOMUS isn't directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We don't knowingly collect their data; if we learn we have, we delete it.

International readers

LOOMUS is global and works in six languages. Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate, with appropriate safeguards.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the product or by email.

This page is written to be readable, not to be a maze. It reflects how LOOMUS actually works today; if anything here is unclear, just ask — sophie@loomus.ai.
