Pasquino · 1501

"Si tace il libro, parla il muro."

In Rome, when the book stays silent, the wall speaks. Citizens have pinned claims, satire, and prayers to public walls for two thousand years. This wall continues that. Your inscription joins one of the oldest forms of saying something on record.
