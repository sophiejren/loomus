# The Stanford Issue — the Farm's AI rooms, and which doors actually open

*Issue 02 · The Bay Digest · A weekly letter from LOOMUS · June 6, 2026*

---

Thirty-five miles south of the city sits the densest square mile of AI talent on earth — and most of its rooms never show up in your Luma feed. This issue maps **Stanford and the Peninsula**: the free weekly seminars, the closed-door founder dinners, the hackathons that crown the year. Same lens as always — plotted by intimacy and cadence — then a matrix that answers the only question that matters: **what do I leave with?**

📅 **A new issue lands every Saturday morning** — same lens, fresh rooms.

One note on timing: it's June, and Stanford runs on a quarter system. The weekly term-time rooms (HAI seminars, ETL, the GSB AI Club) thin out over summer and return in full when autumn quarter opens in late September. The flagship summits are annual — each is marked with its next date. Live calendar: [loomus.ai/events](https://loomus.ai/events).

## § 01 · The Room Map

*Every dot is one room. Vertical axis = intimacy (how many people in the room). Horizontal axis = cadence (how often it happens). Five archetype zones color-coded.*

> 📌 **Upload the Room Map image here** — file: `og-bay-digest-02.png` from /blog/ (1200×630). Substack supports drag-and-drop for images.

**The 5 archetypes:**

- ⬛ **Marquee Hall** — 1000+ people · annual flagships
- 🟧 **Workshop** — 50–200 people · recurring builds
- 🟨 **Salon** — 20–50 people · monthly dinners
- 🟩 **Lab** — 10–30 people · weekly research
- ⚪ **Lobby** — drop-in · casual

**Reading the map**: Stanford's signature is the dense *weekly column* on the left — HAI, the Digital Economy Lab, ETL, the AI Club, all free and all public if you know they exist. The flagship *halls* stack the upper-right, but they fire only once a year. The quietest, highest-leverage rooms are the *salons* in the middle band — GSB AI Club dinners, AGI House, StartX — closed-door, merit-gated, and where the actual introductions happen.

## § 02 · What you actually leave with

Different rooms produce different outputs. Pick the column that matches what you need — co-founder, customer, hire, paper, idea, or just a useful connection.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **⚪ Lobby** *drop-in · casual* | LOOMUS Builders Breakfast | — | Stanford AI Club Meetups | LOOMUS Deep Reading Club | — | Stanford AI Club Meetups |

**Reading the matrix, Stanford-style**: the *labs* are the Farm's superpower — three free, public, weekly seminars that put you in a room with the people writing next year's citations. The *salons* remain the co-founder engine, but at Stanford they're gated by an accelerator badge or a GSB email. *Halls* fire once a year and are worth flying in for. And TreeHacks is the rare hall that doubles as a hiring floor — a thousand of the best student builders on earth, in one gym, for thirty-six hours.

> *At Stanford the best rooms aren't sold — they're unlocked. The map just tells you which key to cut.*

## ⬛ Archetype 01 · Marquee Hall (1000+ · annual)

Stanford's flagship convenings — the once-a-year rooms where the whole field shows up.

**🗓 Feb 2027 — TreeHacks** · Huang Engineering Center · 1,000+ builders · 36 hrs · Free, selective · → Hire
The largest collegiate hackathon in the U.S. — ~1,000 hackers from 15,000+ applicants. The 2026 edition opened with a Sam Altman keynote and awarded $500,000 in prizes.

**🗓 Jan 2027 — Stanford GSB AI Conference** · Oberndorf Event Center · → Connection
The GSB AI Club's annual flagship; the January 2026 edition sold out with a guest waitlist.

**🗓 Feb, annual — AI+Education Summit** · Stanford HAI × Accelerator for Learning · → Idea
Fourth year. The 2026 theme: *The AI Inflection Point — What, How, and Why We Learn.*

**🗓 May, annual — AI+Science: Accelerating Discovery** · Stanford HAI · → Paper
An interdisciplinary HAI conference separating breakthrough from hype across physics, chemistry, biology, and neuroscience.

**🗓 Jun, annual — RAISE Health Symposium** · Stanford Medicine × HAI · → Connection
Leaders from science, medicine, technology, and policy on deploying AI in biomedicine responsibly. The 2026 edition ran June 2.

**🗓 Oct, annual — Hoffman-Yee Symposium** · Stanford HAI · → Paper
HAI's annual research symposium showcasing its Hoffman-Yee grant recipients.
