The Stanford Issue — the Farm's AI rooms, and which doors actually open.

By Sophie Ren · Founder, LOOMUS · June 6, 2026 Subscribe weekly → 13 min · 1 chart · 1 matrix

Thirty-five miles south of the city sits the densest square mile of AI talent on earth — and most of its rooms never show up in your Luma feed. This issue maps Stanford and the Peninsula: the weekly seminars, the closed-door founder dinners, the hackathons that crown the year. Same lens as always — plotted by intimacy and cadence, then a matrix that answers the only question that matters: what do I leave with?

Weekly A new issue lands every Saturday morning — same lens, fresh rooms. See all issues →

One note on timing: it's June, and Stanford runs on a quarter system — the weekly term-time rooms (HAI seminars, ETL, the GSB AI Club) thin out over summer and return in full force when autumn quarter opens in late September. The flagship summits are annual; we've marked each with its next date. Submissions: events@loomus.ai. Live calendar: loomus.ai/events.

§ 01 The Room Map

Every dot is one event. Vertical axis = intimacy (how many people in the room). Horizontal axis = cadence (how often it happens). Five archetype zones color-coded.

Reading the map: Stanford's signature is the dense weekly column on the left — HAI, the Digital Economy Lab, ETL, the AI Club, all free and all public if you know they exist. The flagship halls stack the upper-right, but they fire only once a year. The quietest, highest-leverage rooms are the salons in the middle band — GSB AI Club dinners, AGI House, StartX — closed-door, merit-gated, and where the actual introductions happen.

Reading the matrix, Stanford-style: the labs are the Farm's superpower — three free, public, weekly seminars that put you in a room with the people writing next year's citations. The salons remain the co-founder engine, but at Stanford they're gated by an accelerator badge or a GSB email. Halls fire once a year and are worth flying in for. And TreeHacks is the rare hall that doubles as a hiring floor — a thousand of the best student builders on earth, in one gym, for thirty-six hours.

At Stanford the best rooms aren't sold — they're unlocked. The map just tells you which key to cut.

Archetype 01 · 1000+ · Annual
Marquee Hall

Stanford's flagship convenings — the once-a-year rooms where the whole field shows up. They fire infrequently, so you plan the year around them.

Feb2027

TreeHacks 2027

Huang Engineering Center · 1,000+ builders · 36 hrs · Free · selective Hire

The largest collegiate hackathon in the U.S. — roughly 1,000 hackers selected from 15,000+ applicants. The 2026 edition opened with a Sam Altman keynote and awarded $500,000 in prizes. The single best place to recruit student builders all year.

Jan2027

Stanford GSB · AI Conference

Oberndorf Event Center, GSB · One day · 2026 sold out Connection

The GSB AI Club's annual flagship — a full day of operators, founders, and investors on the business of AI. The January 2026 edition sold out with a guest waitlist. Register the day tickets open.

Febannual

AI+Education Summit

Stanford HAI × Accelerator for Learning · One day Idea

Now in its fourth year, the summit convenes researchers, educators, policymakers, and philanthropic partners on how AI is reshaping how we learn. The 2026 theme: The AI Inflection Point — What, How, and Why We Learn.

Mayannual

AI+Science: Accelerating Discovery

Stanford HAI · One day Paper

An interdisciplinary HAI conference spanning physics, chemistry, biology, and neuroscience — examining where AI is enabling genuine breakthroughs versus where the hype outruns the science. The most rigorous AI-and-science room in the Bay.

Junannual

RAISE Health Symposium

Stanford Medicine × HAI · One day Connection

The annual convening of the RAISE Health initiative — leaders from science, medicine, technology, and policy on deploying AI in biomedicine responsibly. The 2026 edition ran June 2. Where clinical AI and frontier research actually meet.

Octannual

Hoffman-Yee Symposium

Stanford HAI · One day · Public Paper

HAI's annual research symposium showcasing its Hoffman-Yee grant recipients — the institute's biggest interdisciplinary bets, presented as posters and panels. An early read on the research that gets cited two years out.

Archetype 02 · 20–50 · Monthly
The Salon

The co-founder engine — at Stanford, gated by an accelerator badge, a GSB email, or a merit application. Smaller, harder to enter, and the rooms where the introductions that matter actually happen.

Monthly2026

AGI House · Founder Dinners

Hillsborough · Merit-based · Free Co-founder

The original AI founder house — a Hillsborough mansion running dinners, fireside chats, and hackathons for builders and researchers. Access is merit-based and they never charge for community events. The Peninsula's densest founder network, twenty minutes from campus.

MonthlyTerm-time

Stanford GSB · AI Club Firesides & BBLs

Stanford GSB · Closed-door Idea

The GSB AI Club's closed-door fireside chats and brown-bag lunches bring founders and senior leaders of AI-native companies to campus — recent guests include leaders from OpenAI, Mercor, Manus, Nvidia, and Genspark. Fifteen-plus events a year; the off-record version of the news.

Monthly2026

StartX · Founder Dinners

Stanford-affiliated accelerator · Members Co-founder

StartX is Stanford's non-profit founder community and accelerator. Its dinners and member sessions are a tight, vetted room of Stanford-affiliated founders — the alumni network turned into a working table. Apply with a Stanford affiliation.

MonthlyVol 7+ LOOMUS host

LOOMUS · Founders Salon

Palo Alto & SF · Closed-door Co-founder

LOOMUS's invite-only salon series, reopening Q3 2026 with a Palo Alto edition. Sophie hosts in person; the agenda is set 72 hours ahead by attendees themselves. Apply via events@loomus.ai.

▢
Archetype 03 · 50–200 · Recurring
Workshop

Where prototypes ship and pitches get made. The Stanford build rooms run hot in term and around demo season — show up with something half-finished.

Mayannual

Stanford Founders · Demo Day '26

Stanford · 300+ investors · Angel / pre-seed / seed+ Customer

The annual demo day where Stanford-affiliated startups pitch to 300+ investors across angel, pre-seed, and seed+ tracks. The 2026 edition ran May 7 with applications due in April. The cleanest on-ramp from Stanford founder to a first check.

Monthly2026

AGI House · Saturday Build Day

Hillsborough · Selected builders · Free Co-founder

The residence-style hackathon that started it all — a couple dozen builders per Saturday with frontier-lab engineers on hand as mentors. Themed around whatever shipped that week. The fastest way to find someone to build with on the Peninsula.

Term-time2026

Stanford GSB · AI Club Build & Panel Nights

Stanford GSB · Open to Stanford Hire

Beyond the closed firesides, the GSB AI Club runs open panels, workshops, and demo nights — the public face of its fifteen-plus-events-a-year calendar. The easiest GSB AI room to walk into without an invite.

WeeklyTerm-time

Stanford AI Club · Project Nights

Stanford campus · Student-run · Free Hire

The undergraduate AI club's working nights — build sessions, paper readings, and project showcases run by the students who'll start the next wave of companies. Where you meet the 21-year-olds before a recruiter does.

Archetype 04 · 10–30 · Weekly
The Lab

Stanford's superpower. Three free, public, weekly seminars put you in a room with the people writing next year's citations — if you know they exist. Most run during term and pause over summer.

WeeklyTerm · Wed

Stanford HAI · Seminar Series

Stanford campus · Public · Free Paper

HAI's weekly seminar — frontier-lab researchers, policy fellows, and Stanford faculty. Recent talks ranged from how people actually use ChatGPT to AI and U.S.–China strategic stability. Recordings post, but the in-room Q&A is the value.

WeeklyTerm

Stanford · Digital Economy Lab Seminar

Stanford campus · Public · Free Paper

Erik Brynjolfsson's Digital Economy Lab runs a seminar on the economics of AI — productivity, labor markets, the supply and demand for intelligence. Heavier on data and measurement than most rooms. Where the AI-and-jobs debate gets numbers.

WeeklyTerm

Stanford ETL · Entrepreneurial Thought Leaders

Stanford STVP · eCorner · Public · Free Idea

The Stanford Technology Ventures Program's weekly speaker series — leading founders on building companies, with talks published free on eCorner. Recent guests include the CEOs of Gamma and Mill. The most-listened-to startup lecture series in the world, and it's open.

WeeklyTerm

Stanford AI Lab · SAIL Seminars

Gates Computer Science · Public · Free Hire

The Stanford AI Lab's research seminars and talks — the technical core of the Farm's AI output, from robotics to NLP to systems. Underrated for sourcing PhD talent before it enters the founder pipeline.

Quarterly2026 LOOMUS co-host

Stanford × LOOMUS · AI × Education Roundtable

Stanford campus · Closed · Half-day Paper

A half-day roundtable bringing AI researchers, education leaders, and EdTech founders to one table. The 2024 session yielded a published whitepaper; the 2026 session targets AI literacy and the role of curated knowledge. Co-hosted with LOOMUS.
