A few honest questions.

Is Marginalia really free forever?

Yes. Daily picks, knowledge graphs, your shelf, your /you — all free, no trial, no cap. We're a foundation, not a startup. The curation is the gift.

What does "all current and future tools" mean?

Marginalia is our first tool. More are coming. Each one auto-unlocks for Patrons the day it ships — no price change, ever. Like Apple One, for learning.

Is 501(c)(3) status active?

Application filed · IRS approval pending (typical 3–12 months). Once granted, US Benefactor donations become fully tax-deductible — retroactively to today.

Why no events, no Discord, no calls?

LOOMUS is a one-person + AI operation. We'd rather Sophie spend that time making the product better. If readers eventually want gatherings, we'll listen. For now: the work is the relationship.

Just want to use Marginalia once?

No commitment — pay-as-you-go at distill.loomus.ai. $3.99 a distill, $6.99 a knowledge graph, $7.99 both for the same piece (save $2.99). Each one-off adds $1 to the education fund.
