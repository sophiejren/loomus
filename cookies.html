Cookie Policy

Last updated · 9 June 2026

We use the fewest cookies we can — enough to keep you signed in and to understand what's working. No advertising cookies, no ad networks, no selling your attention.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a site stores in your browser. Some are necessary for the site to function; others help us understand usage.

The cookies we use

TypeWhat it doesNeeded?
EssentialKeeps you signed in and your session secure (e.g. after entering your six-digit code).Required
PreferencesRemembers small choices, like your language.Optional
AnalyticsGoogle Analytics — anonymous-ish usage data so we can see which pages help readers.Optional

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may mean you can't stay signed in. Analytics can also be limited with a browser opt-out or extensions like Google's Analytics Opt-out.

Consent & changes

Where the law requires it, we'll ask for your consent to non-essential cookies. If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
